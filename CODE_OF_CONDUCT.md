# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We aim to make Heimdall a respectful, practical, and safe project for AppSec, DevSecOps, and research users.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but be specific when reporting problems.
- Avoid sharing private vulnerabilities, secrets, or production target details.
- Keep discussion focused on safe testing and improving the tool.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Posting real secrets or private exploit details.
- Encouraging unauthorized testing.
- Misrepresenting Heimdall as production-ready when discussing this alpha release.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and issues that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may lead to blocked participation.
